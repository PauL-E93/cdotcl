What is playschool?

Playschool introduces young children to learning through play. In a caring environment, children practice interacting with others, expressing themselves, and becoming more independent.

  • Play and hands-on activities encourage curiosity and creativity.
  • Shared activities help children practice taking turns, communicating, and making friends.
  • Simple routines help children feel comfortable in a learning setting.

What is preschool?

Preschool builds a foundation for formal schooling through guided activities and hands-on discovery. Children develop early learning skills while continuing to learn through play.

  • Early literacy activities introduce listening, language, and letters.
  • Number activities help children explore counting, patterns, and basic concepts.
  • Art and group activities encourage creativity, cooperation, and confidence.

What is tutoring?

Tutoring provides focused learning support for students who need help understanding lessons or strengthening their skills. One-on-one or small-group guidance gives students opportunities to ask questions and practice with encouragement.

  • Guided explanations help students work through lessons they find challenging.
  • Practice activities support understanding and reinforce learning.
  • Personalized support helps students build confidence and work toward their learning goals.
